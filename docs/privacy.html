Privacy Policy – Linkedin to Sheet

Last updated: November 17, 2026

“Linkedin to Sheet” (the “extension”) is a browser extension for Firefox and Chrome that reads the company information displayed on a LinkedIn company page and saves it, at the user's request, as a row in a Google Sheet that belongs to the user. This policy explains what data the extension handles and how.

Summary

  • The extension has no server. The developer does not receive, collect or store any of your data.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry of any kind.
  • Your data goes only to Google's APIs, to your own Google Sheet, when you click Save.

Data the extension handles

Website content (LinkedIn company pages)

When you open the extension popup on a LinkedIn company page, the extension reads the company information visible on that page (name, website, LinkedIn URL, industry, company size, location) to pre-fill a form. This happens only on LinkedIn pages and only when you open the popup. You can edit every field, and add a free-text note, before saving. Nothing is sent anywhere until you click Save.

Authentication information (Google sign-in)

When you click “Connect Google account”, you sign in through Google's standard OAuth 2.0 consent screen. The extension never sees your Google password. It receives an access token and a refresh token, which it stores locally in the browser's extension storage (storage.local) so you stay connected. It requests the narrowest scope it needs, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which limits access to the files you create or explicitly open with the extension.

Settings

The spreadsheet ID and the sheet tab name you choose are stored locally in the browser's extension storage.

How the data is used

  • The page content is used only to pre-fill the form and, once you click Save, to write a row in your Google Sheet through the Google Sheets API.
  • The tokens are used only to authenticate requests to Google's APIs (sheets.googleapis.com and oauth2.googleapis.com).
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to determine creditworthiness.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs by Linkedin to Sheet will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage, retention and deletion

All data is stored on your device. Click “Disconnect” in the extension options page to delete the Google tokens, or uninstall the extension to delete everything it stores. You can also revoke the extension's access at any time in your Google Account permissions. Rows already written to your Google Sheet remain in your spreadsheet, which you control.

Permissions

  • storage: keep your settings and Google tokens locally.
  • identity: run the Google sign-in flow.
  • activeTab: read the URL of the tab you are on when you open the popup.
  • Access to linkedin.com: read the company information on LinkedIn company pages.
  • Access to sheets.googleapis.com and oauth2.googleapis.com: write to your Google Sheet and manage the sign-in tokens.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly handle their data.
